# Business Growth & Marketing Challenge

**Overview:** A strategic marketing initiative connecting Tobb Technologies with local educational institutions to provide affordable digital services to small businesses using student talent.

**Purpose:** To generate a new revenue stream, build brand awareness, and create a sustainable pipeline for junior talent acquisition.

**Target Audience:** 
1. Local small businesses needing affordable web and digital marketing services.
2. IT and marketing students seeking Work Integrated Learning (WIL) hours.

**Business Value:** Positions Tobb Technologies as a community leader while capturing budget-conscious clients and testing potential future hires at low risk.
