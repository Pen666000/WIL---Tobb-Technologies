# Connecting Student Talent with Real Business Opportunities

**1. The Opportunity**
There are two gaps in the market:
* Small businesses need affordable websites, marketing materials, and digital solutions but often cannot afford traditional agencies.
* Students studying IT, software development, and marketing need practical experience, real projects, and opportunities to complete their Work Integrated Learning (WIL) requirements.
Tobb Technologies has an opportunity to bring these two groups together.

**2. The Solution: The Tobb Talent Bridge**
Tobb Technologies will establish a specialised service offering where student teams work on real projects for small businesses at affordable prices to help Tobb grow.
Services will include:
* Website development.
* Branding and promotional material.
* Digital marketing.
* Application prototypes and MVP development.
Tobb will source clients, organise student teams, coordinate projects, and provide guidance throughout the process. Our focus is to make digital services simple, affordable, and reliable.

**3. Student Recruitment and Partnerships**
Tobb will establish relationships with universities, TVET colleges, and skills development programmes to identify students who need practical industry experience.
Students will be selected according to their skills and matched with suitable projects.
This creates a continuous pipeline of emerging talent while helping students gain exposure to real business environments.

**4. Project Delivery**
Projects will be completed through structured short-term engagements, including weekend project sprints and agreed project timelines.
Students will work in teams, combining development, design, and marketing skills.
Tobb will oversee project coordination, client communication, and technical reviews to ensure a structured delivery process.

**5. Student Earnings and Rewards**
An important part of this initiative is allowing students to benefit financially from the value they create.
The proposed model includes:
* **Project Payments:** Students receive an agreed portion of the revenue from successfully completed and paid client projects they contributed to.
* **Performance Rewards:** Additional incentives may be offered for exceptional contributions.
* **Revenue Sharing:** Students may receive an agreed share of revenue from selected products they help develop and commercialise.
* **Equity Opportunities:** For selected projects that grow into successful businesses, Tobb may explore offering contributing students an ownership stake.
Payment for ordinary client projects will be linked to successful project completion and client payment. Students will not automatically receive payment simply for registering or participating.

**6. Business Value to Tobb Technologies**
The initiative creates several opportunities for Tobb to promote its services and attract new clients:
* **Additional Revenue:** A new income stream from small businesses seeking affordable digital services.
* **Brand Growth:** Positive brand exposure through supporting students and local businesses.
* **Talent Acquisition:** An opportunity to identify and work with promising young professionals.
* **Strategic Partnerships:** Stronger relationships with educational institutions.
* **Future Products:** Opportunities to identify, develop, and commercialise promising student projects.

**7. Implementation Steps**
To put this concept into action, Tobb Technologies must execute the following practical steps:
* **Partnership Outreach:** Draft and send introductory proposals to career centres at local TVET colleges, universities, and tech bootcamps offering to host students for Work Integrated Learning.
* **Pilot Project Setup:** Identify three existing or prospective small business clients and offer them heavily discounted web development or digital marketing services in exchange for participating in the inaugural talent bridge sprint.
* **Operational Framework:** Create standardised student registration forms, non-disclosure agreements, and a project management dashboard to coordinate the student teams securely.

**8. Success Measures**
The success of this marketing and growth initiative will be evaluated using the following metrics:
* **Client Acquisition:** Securing at least three paying small business clients within the first quarter of the pilot launch.
* **Academic Placements:** Successfully onboarding and matching 10-15 students to projects to complete their mandatory practical hours.
* **Revenue Generation:** Achieving a positive profit margin on student-led deliverables after compensating the talent and covering Tobb’s project management overheads.

**9. References and External Sources**
This proposal is informed by external observations of South African market conditions and academic structures, separating personal observations from external information:
* **Small Business Digital Demand:** South African small businesses show strong intent to adopt digital tools, with recent data indicating that 52% use AI and digital tools regularly, but 56% require external support and guidance to implement them into operations, demonstrating a clear market for affordable tech consulting (Bizcommunity, 2026). 
* **Work Integrated Learning (WIL) Needs:** South African tertiary institutions mandate Work Integrated Learning for IT and creative students, requiring industry partners to help students apply technical skills to real-world environments and build their CVs before graduation (SAE Institute South Africa, 2026; CPUT, 2026).
